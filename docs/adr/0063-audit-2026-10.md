# ADR-0063: Audit of October 2026 — method, findings, fixes

Status: accepted
Date: 2026-10-20

## Scope and method

Every dimension of the code base after ADR-0050..0062: money arithmetic, permissions and data exposure, SQL and
input handling, secrets, the second phone's replication, backups, swallowed errors, the app's threading and crash
risks, and speed on a large data set. Each check is either a repeatable test or a scan whose result is recorded here.

1. **Randomized invariant test** (`InvariantFuzzTest`, new): three fixed seeds × 150 operations in CI (`SABOU_FUZZ`
   for more; 3 × 1,500 run for this audit) of purchases (paid now, partly, later), cash and credit sales with
   discounts, collections, supplier payments, waste, reversals of sales and invoices. After every operation: the
   trial balance nets to zero; the inventory account equals the stock's value; receivables, payables and the cash
   box equal their sub-ledgers. At the end: the audit chain verifies; scorecard, profit bridge (steps = change),
   price what-if, cash forecast and usage read the whole history. Refusals must be domain refusals.
2. **Scans**: floating point and integer division on money; public reads without a permission check; SQL built from
   strings; logging; secret storage; Android backup flags; database work on the UI thread; `single()`/`first()` on
   possibly empty lists; spreadsheet formula injection.
3. **Volume benchmark** (90 days of a busy restaurant, in CI): the scorecard (see ADR-0062, hardening).
4. **Second phone**: records, actions and settings made on the second phone leave both databases identical.

## Open observation

One full local run of the suite (out of 13 in a row) reported a single failure whose name was not recorded; the next
12 runs passed. The likeliest candidate is `SyncLinkTest` (real sockets with fixed timeouts) under machine load. It is
left unchanged until it shows again; CI names any failure.

## Not covered

A real device with the restaurant's own data, and the Sepand export formats beyond the samples in the tests.
